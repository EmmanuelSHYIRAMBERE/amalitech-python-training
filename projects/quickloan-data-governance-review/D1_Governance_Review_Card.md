**Prepared by:** Emmanuel Shyirambere | **Date:** May 2026

## Overview

This Governance Review Card documents three distinct risk findings and one reporting recommendation resulting from a structured review of QuickLoan Mobile's data pipeline. Findings are grounded in Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and core data governance principles.

---

## Section 1 — Data Quality Risk

| Field                          | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :----------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Severity**                   | HIGH RISK                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Issue / Definition**         | **Incomplete and Inconsistent Customer Data.** Records entering the pipeline contain missing required fields (null phone numbers, empty income fields) and conflicting formats: dates appear as both `DD/MM/YYYY` and `MM-DD-YY`; phone numbers appear as both `024XXXXXXX` and `+23324XXXXXXX`. The Raw Data DB (`AllEvents`) stores every record with no validation gate, allowing malformed data to persist undetected.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Impact**                     | Dirty records propagate directly into the Preprocessing Service and LoanScore ML Model. The model's null-handling logic silently converts missing values into default scores, artificially depressing creditworthiness ratings for applicants whose data was malformed at entry — not because they represent genuine credit risk. This inflates rejection rates unfairly and degrades model reliability over time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Suggested Fix / Mitigation** | **1. API Gateway Validation (Immediate):** Enforce server-side schema validation at Step 2. Reject or quarantine records missing required fields before they reach the Raw Data DB. Mandatory fields: national ID, phone (E.164 format), income declaration, loan amount. **2. Canonical Format Contract:** Define and publish an API input specification enforcing ISO 8601 for all date fields and E.164 for all phone numbers. **3. Data Quality Score:** Attach a computed `dq_score` (completeness percentage) to every ingested record. Records below 80% completeness are routed to a human review queue before ML scoring proceeds. |

## Section 2 — Legal & Compliance Risk

| Field                               | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :---------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Severity**                        | CRITICAL                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Issue / Definition**              | **No Explicit Consent and Excessive PII Collection.** The app collects the user's full contact list, continuous GPS location history, and device event logs at the point of onboarding. None of these data types are necessary for assessing loan eligibility. There is no consent screen, no granular opt-in mechanism, and no consent record stored anywhere in the pipeline. Data flows from the User Mobile App (Step 1) through the API Gateway (Step 2) directly into the Raw Data DB (Step 3) with zero compliance checkpoint.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Data Classification**             | **SENSITIVE** — Contact list and GPS location history constitute sensitive personal data under Act 843. **CONFIDENTIAL** — National ID number, financial records, phone number, and full name. Both tiers require explicit, documented consent prior to collection and processing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Applicable Ghana DPA Violations** | `s.17` Unlawful processing — no documented legal basis for collecting contact lists or GPS data. `s.19` No consent — data is collected and stored before any consent is captured or recorded. `s.20` Data minimization breach — data collected far exceeds the stated processing purpose of credit risk assessment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Impact**                          | Financial penalty of up to GHS 60,000 per violation under Act 843 s.71. Criminal liability for wilful or reckless breaches. Risk of enforcement notice, operational suspension, or public censure by the Data Protection Commission. Severe reputational damage in a trust-sensitive financial services context.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Suggested Fix / Mitigation**      | **1. Consent Layer (Immediate):** Insert a Consent and Validation Layer between the API Gateway (Step 2) and Raw Data DB (Step 3). This layer presents a granular consent screen before any data is stored, records a timestamped consent record per user per data category, and blocks data ingestion for any category where consent is withheld. **2. Data Minimization (Immediate):** Remove collection of contact list and GPS history from the mobile app entirely. Collect only: national ID, mobile money reference, income declaration, and loan amount requested. **3. Appoint a DPO:** Designate a Data Protection Officer and register with Ghana's Data Protection Commission as mandated by Act 843. **4. Retention Schedule:** Define and enforce automatic deletion timelines — SENSITIVE data after 6 months; CONFIDENTIAL after 36 months. |

## Section 3 — Bias and Fairness Risk

| Field                          | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| :----------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Severity**                   | HIGH RISK                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Issue / Definition**         | **Proxy Bias in the LoanScore ML Model with No Decision Transparency.** The model (Step 6) uses features including GPS location, contact list size, and device model as inputs to its credit scoring calculation. These are proxy variables: they do not directly measure creditworthiness but are statistically correlated with protected attributes including geographic region, gender, and socioeconomic class. The Decision Service (Step 7) executes fully automated approvals and rejections with no logging, no human review layer, and no explanation mechanism for declined applicants.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Source of Bias**             | **Historical Bias + Proxy Bias.** The training data reflects historical lending patterns in which rural, female, and lower-income applicants were systemically under-approved. The model has learned these patterns as predictive signals. GPS location encodes region, which encodes income and ethnicity as proxies. Contact list size encodes social capital, which correlates with gender and class. The model therefore discriminates indirectly without explicitly including a protected class as a feature.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Impact**                     | Rural applicants, women, and first-time borrowers receive lower LoanScores driven by structural disadvantage rather than credit risk. The absence of logging means there is no audit trail: regulators cannot investigate complaints, wrongly rejected applicants have no recourse, and the company cannot demonstrate compliance with anti-discrimination obligations under the Ghana Labour Act 2003.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Suggested Fix / Mitigation** | **1. Remove Proxy Features (Immediate):** Exclude GPS location, contact list size, and device model from the ML feature set. Retain only direct creditworthiness indicators: mobile money repayment history, stated income, loan amount, and repayment term requested. **2. Decision Logging (Immediate):** Implement full structured audit logging at Step 7 for every automated decision: applicant ID, model version, feature values, output score, decision, timestamp, and reason code. **3. Human Review Layer:** Route all borderline decisions (scores within 10% of the approval threshold) to a human reviewer before a final outcome is issued. **4. Model Retraining:** Retrain on representative, de-biased training data. Adopt Equalized Odds as the primary fairness constraint metric. |

## Section 4 — Storytelling and Reporting Recommendation

| Field                  | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| :--------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Metric to Monitor**  | **Demographic Approval Rate Disparity (DARD).** Definition: the difference in loan approval rates between the highest-approved demographic group and the lowest-approved demographic group, computed monthly. Formula: `DARD = Approval Rate (highest group) - Approval Rate (lowest group)`. Groups are segmented by gender, region, and income tier. A DARD value exceeding 10 percentage points triggers a mandatory model fairness review before the next scoring batch executes. |
| **Visualization Type** | **Grouped Bar Chart** with one bar per demographic segment showing monthly approval rates side by side. An overlaid horizontal reference line marks the 10-percentage-point alert threshold. A secondary **Line Chart** positioned below tracks the DARD value as a single trend line over rolling 12-month history, making directional movement immediately visible.                                                                                                                 |
| **Why It Matters**     | DARD converts complex model behaviour into a single board-reportable number that executives, regulators, and applicant advocates can interpret without ML expertise, creating an audit-ready evidence trail that demonstrates proactive monitoring of disparate impact under Ghana DPA and emerging African AI governance frameworks.                                                                                                                                                 |
